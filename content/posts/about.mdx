## Uses
### Software
#### Daily
I spend most of my time writing and programming in [Neovim](https://neovim.io/); vibe-coding in [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/) (see [terminal](#terminal)) via [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code); reviewing my flashcards in [Amgi](https://github.com/alisheryeginbay/amgi); browsing in [Helium](https://helium.computer/) (with [uBlock Origin](http://github.com/gorhill/uBlock)).

I use [1Password](https://1password.com/) for managing my passwords.
#### AI
I primarily use [Claude](https://claude.ai) ([Opus 5.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/overview) as of 9 October 2026).
#### Language learning
I use [Yomitan](https://yomitan.wiki/) for vocabulary; [asbplayer](https://github.com/asbplayer/asbplayer) for subtitles. I also use [the Merriam-Webster Thesaurus](https://www.merriam-webster.com/thesaurus).
#### Terminal
I use Ghostty with [fish](https://fishshell.com/) (shell), [Starship](https://starship.rs/) (shell prompt), [eza](https://github.com/eza-community/eza) (alternative to `ls`), [zoxide](https://github.com/ajeetdsouza/zoxide) (smarter `cd`), [bat](https://github.com/sharkdp/bat) (alternative to `cat`).
#### Miscellaneous
I use [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) instead of Spotlight (and you should too!). For managing windows, I use their [Window Management](https://www.raycast.com/core-features/window-management) feature with Rectangle preset.[^1]
