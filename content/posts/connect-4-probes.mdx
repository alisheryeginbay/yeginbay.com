**TL;DR:** I'm training a small transformer on Connect 4 games and probing its activations for the board state, the way [Othello-GPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13382) did for Othello. The training games so far are uniformly random play. What's confusing me: whether Connect 4 is rich enough for a model to bother building a world model at all, and what a strong-play dataset for it would even look like. If you have ideas, [email me](mailto:alisher@yeginbay.com)!

Wherein we explore interpretable world models in Connect 4. [Repository](https://github.com/alisheryeginbay/connect-4).

## Current understanding
A while ago I read the [Othello GPT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13382) [@li2022] and decided that it would be interesting to reproduce it. But I thought that reusing Othello would be a bit boring, so I decided to try the game of [Connect 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connect_Four) instead. Although Connect 4 is much simpler, I think we'll still be able to find something gripping (I hope).
