* Sidenotes

A sidenote sits in the margin on wide screens and opens on tap on narrow ones.[fn:1] Inline notes work too.[fn::An inline note, written right where it's referenced.] A note can hold math and links.[fn:2] A note referenced twice keeps its number the second time, without a second note.[fn:1]

A note can have several paragraphs.[fn:3]

* Math

Inline math such as $W_{QK} = W_Q W_K^T$ and $\alpha + \beta = \beta + \alpha$ sits in the text. Display math gets its own line:

$$
A = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{x_i W_{QK} x_j^T}{\sqrt{d_{\text{head}}}}\right)
$$

Aligned equations, with a matrix:

$$ \begin{aligned}
q &= x_i W_Q \\
k &= x_j W_K \\
\begin{bmatrix} \text{head}_1 & \text{head}_2 & \cdots & \text{head}_{n} \end{bmatrix} W_O &= \sum_{h} \text{head}_h W_O^h
\end{aligned} $$
