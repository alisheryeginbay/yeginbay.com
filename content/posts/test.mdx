## Math

Inline math such as $W_{QK} = W_Q W_K^T$ and $\alpha + \beta = \beta + \alpha$ sits in the text. Display math gets its own line:

$$
A = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{x_i W_{QK} x_j^T}{\sqrt{d_{\text{head}}}}\right)
$$

Aligned equations, with a matrix:

$$
\begin{aligned}
q &= x_i W_Q \\
k &= x_j W_K \\
\begin{bmatrix} \text{head}_1 & \text{head}_2 & \cdots & \text{head}_{n} \end{bmatrix} W_O &= \sum_{h} \text{head}_h W_O^h
\end{aligned}
$$
