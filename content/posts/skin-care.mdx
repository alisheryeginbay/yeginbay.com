These notes cover my skin care routine & a few advices on the topic.

The first thing to point out is that skin care is not that complex! You don't have to use dozens of products every day, or have some kind of esoteric routine. Most of it comes down to 3--4 products.

## Sunscreen

If you walk away with only one insight, let it be this one: use a sunscreen, daily!

It's not only the matter of protecting against sunburns. The sun's UV radiation causes skin cancer, direct DNA damage, and [photoaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoaging).

It's important to choose a good sunscreen.

Essentially only two types of UV radiation reaches the ground: UVA and UVB. Simply put, UVA is largely responsible for aging and UVB for burning and skin cancer. Most of the sunscreens refer to their [SPF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunscreen#Sun_Protection_Factor), sun protection factor. It measures the protection against burning. For example, SPF30 means that only $\frac{1}{30}$ of the burning radiation will reach the skin. The best SPFs are typically labeled as SPF50+.

SPF mostly measures the protection against UVB, while we still have UVA causing the visible aging! So it's crucial to have a sunscreen with UVA protection too. Most of the Korean and other Asian countries' sunscreens include PA labels, which show their UVA protection. PA+ = some, PA++ = moderate, PA+++ = high, PA++++ = very high.

Some tips on sunscreen:
- Reapply it every 2 hours of sun exposure.
- Use roughly 2 finger-length of sunscreen for a single application.
- Don't forget your neck too!
