Typing is integral in my daily life: I write, program, journal, run terminal commands, etc. I've been typing since I was \~9 years old[^1].

However, my typing experience has been driven by casual process of just typing everyday, for many hours. Without using any specific technique or awareness of how can I do better, I've so far built a 97 WPM typing speed[^2].

So in this post I delve into the world of typing: keyboard layouts, what affects the typing speed (besides raw practice), ergonomics. My goal is not increasing my typing speed (though it's a nice bonus) but pure curiosity and potentially improving my typing comfort.
