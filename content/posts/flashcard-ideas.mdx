Fun / practical / social ideas for flashcards: 
- Birthdays
- Fonts
- City facts
- World population, US GDP, the distance to the moon, the number of seconds in a year
- Keyboard shortcuts
- Book ideas
- Camera roll: a random photo from N years ago and the prompt "What was happening here?"
- Open questions:
	- To be added...
- Actions:
	- “Text someone you haven’t talked to in a year”
	- "“Notice one thing on your walk today you’ve never seen”
- Family stories, sayings, and voices: recorded and preserved before they’re gone.

Most of the ideas were suggested by Claude :)
