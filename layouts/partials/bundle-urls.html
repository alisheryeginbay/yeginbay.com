{{- /* A post kept as a page bundle refers to its images by relative path: [[file:images/a.webp]] becomes . Hugo publishes them under the post, at /slug/images/a.webp, but the site is served without trailing slashes, so from /slug the browser resolves the path to /images/a.webp and finds nothing. This makes such paths absolute. Takes a dict with "page" and "content" and returns the content with relative image paths rooted at the page. */ -}} {{- $base := strings.TrimSuffix "/" .page.RelPermalink -}} {{- return (replaceRE `(]*src=")([^"/#:][^":]*)"` (printf `${1}%s/${2}"` $base) .content) -}}
